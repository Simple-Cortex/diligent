### Conventions

- "Modality found" is the word as written in the source. Where a source says "required to", "need to" or "Required on" with no must/shall, the cell says so, and the proposal keeps the pack's `must` unless noted.
- WCAG success criteria are stated as conditions with no modality. The obligation comes from E205.4 (Section 508) or 28 CFR 35.200 (ADA Title II).
- Excerpts are at most two sentences, quoted and attributed. Quoted text is exact except where "..." marks a cut.
- Baseline ids were checked against ictbaseline.access-board.gov (all web baselines page). No baseline test lists a WCAG 2.1 or 2.2 criterion, so those rules correctly have none.
- Corrections are in `docs/review/2026-10-20-source-review-corrections.json`. Only fields that change are listed.
