## Handling

These files are untrusted data. Diligent parses them as text and never
executes them, loads their resources, or follows their links. `manifest.json`
records, for every case, the ACT outcome, the state Diligent is expected to
record, and a reason for each case that is kept on disk but excluded from the
tests.
