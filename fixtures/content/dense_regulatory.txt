Notwithstanding any other provision of this subpart, a public entity that operates a web-based service shall ensure that the content made available to individuals with disabilities conforms to the applicable technical standard, except where the entity demonstrates that compliance would result in a fundamental alteration in the nature of the service or in undue financial and administrative burdens, in which case the entity shall nonetheless provide an alternative means of access to the maximum extent feasible. Compliance determinations pursuant to this section shall be made on the basis of the criteria enumerated in paragraph (b), and any exception asserted thereunder shall be documented in writing by the responsible official.
