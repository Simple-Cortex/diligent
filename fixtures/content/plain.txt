Residents can renew a library card online or at any branch. Online renewal is the faster option, and most people finish in about five minutes. To begin, open the library website and select the renewal page. Enter your card number and the personal identification number you chose when you signed up. If you cannot remember that number, call the help desk and a librarian will reset it for you. After the renewal goes through, you will see a confirmation on the screen and get a message by email. Your renewed card stays valid for two years. Library staff answer questions on weekdays from nine to five.
