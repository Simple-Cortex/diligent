Eligibility determinations

Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (b), the administrative determination regarding eligibility for supplemental reimbursement shall be contingent upon the demonstrable satisfaction of all applicable documentation requirements prescribed by the appropriate regulatory authority.

Applications are reviewed by the designated committee, and determinations are communicated to applicants following the completion of the statutory evaluation procedures that are established under the implementing regulations.

Any representation that is submitted in connection with a request for reconsideration must be accompanied by substantiating evidence, and the supplementary materials are considered only when they have been received within the prescribed interval.

The responsible official is authorized to promulgate additional interpretive guidance concerning the administration of the program, provided that such guidance is consistent with the governing statutory framework.
