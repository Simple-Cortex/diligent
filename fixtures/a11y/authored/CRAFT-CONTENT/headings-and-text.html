Guide

Step one

You can apply online. Fill in the short form. We will send you an answer in ten days.

If you need help, call us. Our staff will help you fill in the form. You can also visit an office.

Bring your photo ID. We open at nine. We close at five. Most people finish in ten minutes.

Step two

Pick a date and time that works for you. We will hold your spot for one week.

Tell us if your address changes. We will update your file the same day.

Check your mail for a letter from us. It has your case number. Keep it safe.
